## Harmonization

Train 2012-01 to 2021-12, validation 2022-01 to 2024-12, final test 2025-01 to 2025-12. Method chosen on validation only: **Seasonal ratio with low-count smoothing**.

| Method | Validation WAPE | Test WAPE | Test MAE | Test bias |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Seasonal ratio with low-count smoothing | 66.7% | 38.1% | 7.34 | 1.3% |
| Log-linear regression with calendar-month effects | 89.2% | 49.9% | 9.6 | 33.1% |
| Raw MODIS count, no adjustment (baseline) | 79.8% | 73.5% | 14.15 | -73.3% |

Chosen method on the test year: WAPE 38.1%, 48 evaluation months, 17 with fewer than 5 detections, 5 with none. Evaluation unit: division-months: sum of district estimates compared with observed VIIRS S-NPP.

From the first complete VIIRS S-NPP month, harmonizedCount is the observed VIIRS count. Earlier months are estimates from MODIS Terra + Aqua (Terra only before July 2002).

Limitations: Counts are satellite hotspot detections, not distinct fires. Estimates are aggregate monthly counts, not new detections at exact places. Months before July 2002 use Terra only, so estimates there are likely low. Small counts have large relative error.

## Data files

All files are in `site/data/` (demo data with the same shapes in `site/data/mock/`, `mode: "mock"`). `null` means not available (the sensor did not cover the whole month); `0` means observed with no detections.

- `meta.json`: mode, source, generatedAt, coverage {start, end}, lastCompleteMonth, areas [{id, name, type, division?, verified}], defaultArea, sensorProducts [{id, sensor, processing, coverage, url, request, rows}], methodVersion
- `areas.geojson`: district polygons, properties {id, name, division}, WGS84 lon/lat, 4 decimals
- `calendar/<area id with : replaced by _>.json`: areaId, name, type, months, harmonizedCount, rawModisCount, rawViirsCount, seasonalMedian, anomalyScore (robust z-score, capped at +/-10), status (observed | estimated | missing), units, reference {completeYears, yearCount, excludesTargetYear, minimumYears}
- `map/<year>.json`: year, cell, sourceView, cells [{lon, lat, month, count, sensor, processing}] (observed detections in 0.05-degree cells)
- `harmonize.json`: methodVersion, method, methodName, chosenOn, finalTestYear, metrics {MAE, WAPE, biasPct, n, sparseMonths, zeroMonths}, trainWindow, validationWindow, testWindow, evaluationUnit, metricDefinitions, methods [{id, name, params, validation, test}], uncertainty, continuity, notes
- `manifest.json`: every file with bytes, sha256 and coverage
